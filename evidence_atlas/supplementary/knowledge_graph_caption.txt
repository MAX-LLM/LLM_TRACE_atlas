Supplementary figure: Interactive evidence knowledge graph for large language model studies using electronic health records. Nodes represent structured evidence entities, including dataset-validation levels, clinical-translation stages, dataset provenance, model families, use modes, clinical specialties, trial and human evidence, safety evaluations, governance, and outcomes. Edges indicate that entities were reported in the same included study, and edge width represents the number of linked reports. A model--access edge therefore denotes co-reporting and does not assign that access category intrinsically to every model in a multi-model report. Filters permit exploration by publication year and the two primary evidence dimensions. The downloadable bipartite graph contains 3,209 study and entity nodes and 31,544 study-to-entity edges. Classifications are based on titles, abstracts, and bibliographic metadata.
