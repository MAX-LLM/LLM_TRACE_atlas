After screening and evidence profiling were complete, we compiled the structured study records into a deterministic evidence atlas without additional model inference. Each included report was represented as a study node and linked to controlled entity nodes for dataset validation, clinical translation, dataset provenance, models, model access, use modes, specialties, named public datasets, human and trial evidence, synthetic-data use, safety, oversight, and outcomes. The interactive overview displays selected entity-to-entity co-occurrences aggregated through shared study records; these edges denote co-reporting rather than causal or semantic relations. Stable record identifiers connect every aggregate count to a study page, and CSV, JSON, and GraphML exports preserve the complete study-to-entity graph for independent analysis. Free-text Other responses were grouped only in the overview, while their original study-level text was retained.
