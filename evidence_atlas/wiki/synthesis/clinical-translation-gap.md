# Clinical translation gap

The main evidence gap is the limited intersection between broader validation and evaluation in clinical use. Only **58 reports (3.1%)** combined Level 2 or Level 3 validation with Stage C evidence. Only **5 (0.3%)** combined mixed public and local validation with Stage C evidence.

These dimensions should remain separate. Multi-dataset retrospective validation informs transportability, whereas integrated or interventional evaluation informs clinical use. Neither dimension can substitute for the other.

---
The two primary profile labels use titles and abstracts with targeted full-text corrections; descriptive fields, including trial evidence, use titles and abstracts. Full texts should be consulted before clinical or policy inference.
