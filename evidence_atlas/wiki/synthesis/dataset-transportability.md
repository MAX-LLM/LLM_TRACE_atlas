# Dataset transportability

At least **569 reports (30.4%)** used only local or institutional EHR data. Dataset provenance was unclear in a further **881 (47.1%)**.

Local data provide clinical realism but do not, without independently sourced evaluation, establish transportability across documentation systems, patient populations, languages, or health systems. Public datasets improve reproducibility but can also concentrate evidence within a small number of institutions.

---
The two primary profile labels use titles and abstracts with targeted full-text corrections; descriptive fields, including trial evidence, use titles and abstracts. Full texts should be consulted before clinical or policy inference.
