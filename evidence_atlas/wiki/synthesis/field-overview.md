# Field overview

The atlas contains **1,870 included reports** published from 2022 to June 1, 2026. Of these, **1,803 (96.4%)** were published from 2024 onwards. The evidence base is therefore recent and expanding rapidly.

Evidence maturity has not increased at the same rate as publication volume. **1,051 (56.2%)** reports evaluated one EHR dataset, cohort, institution, or health system, and **1,726 (92.3%)** were retrospective or offline. Only **144 (7.7%)** reported prospective, user-facing, integrated, interventional, or outcome-impact evaluation.
