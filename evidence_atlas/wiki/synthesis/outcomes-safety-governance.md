# Outcomes, safety, and governance

Model accuracy or task performance was reported in **1,747 reports (93.4%)**, whereas patient outcomes or experience were reported in **46 (2.5%)**. Oversight or governance was not described in **1,814 (97.0%)**, and safety evaluation was not described in **1,529 (81.8%)**.

Absence from a title or abstract does not establish absence from the study. These fields identify reporting gaps and candidates for targeted full-text review.

---
The two primary profile labels use titles and abstracts with targeted full-text corrections; descriptive fields, including trial evidence, use titles and abstracts. Full texts should be consulted before clinical or policy inference.
